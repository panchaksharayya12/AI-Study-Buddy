## Slide 2: The Academic Retention Crisis

**The Problem:** Traditional higher education study materials suffer from severe cognitive retention loss when consumed through passive reading.

| Metric | Challenge | Real-World Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **70% Loss** | Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve | Over 70% of lecture materials and textbook chapters are forgotten within 24 hours without systematic retrieval practice. |
| **15% Efficiency** | Passive Highlighting Trap | Highlighting and re-reading produce an illusion of competence without triggering synaptic consolidation or conceptual proof mastery. |
| **4.2 Apps Avg** | Tooling Fragmentation | Students juggle disconnected apps for PDFs, notes, timers, and decks, degrading focus and introducing heavy context-switching friction. |

---

## Slide 3: Unified Solution Architecture

**The Solution:** An active recall cognitive loop that unifies document ingestion, neural concept extraction, tactile spaced repetition, Socratic tutoring, and performance analytics into a single responsive interface.

| Step | Phase | Functionality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 01** | **Ingest** | Upload raw notes, syllabi, or textbook text. The client-side parser extracts definitions, formulas, and structural hierarchy. |
| **Step 02** | **Synthesize** | Generate executive summaries, 3D spaced repetition cards, and adaptive multiple-choice questions automatically. |
| **Step 03** | **Reinforce** | Flip 3D cards using Leitner algorithms, complete timed quizzes, and converse with the personal Socratic AI Tutor. |
| **Step 04** | **Verify** | Review daily retention heatmaps, domain mastery percentage gauges, and unlockable achievement milestones. |

## Slide 5: Socratic Ingestion & AI Tutor

- **Multiformat Chapter Parsing:** Ingests raw text, slides, and notes into structured chapters (Neural Networks, Internet Protocols, Life Sciences) with immediate difficulty tagging.
- **Socratic Mentorship Dialogue:** Instead of providing direct answers, the AI Tutor prompts students with guided questions, step-by-step mathematical proofs, and everyday analogies.
- **Autosaved Smart Notes:** Collapsible accordions with instant clipboard copying, executive summaries, and debounced autosaving for personal revisions.

---

## Slide 6: 3D Spaced Repetition & Adaptive Quizzing

### 3D Tactile Flashcards
- **Hardware-Accelerated CSS 3D Transforms:** 1200px perspective with 180-degree smooth backface visibility flipping.
- **4-Tier Leitner Response Ratings:** `Again`, `Hard`, `Good`, `Easy` dynamically adjusting repeat intervals.
- **Full Keyboard Navigation:** Spacebar to flip, Arrow keys to traverse, Number keys (1-4) for instant rating.

### Adaptive Quizzing Engine
- **Dynamically Generated Exam Bank:** Multiple-choice exams tailored to ingested chapter definitions and formulas.
- **Real-Time Scoring:** Tracks accuracy percentages, completed question counts, and active test time.
- **Contextual Answer Explanations:** Instant feedback highlighting correct choices and contextual justifications for every incorrect option.

---

## Slide 7: Circular Pomodoro & Retention Heatmaps

- **Precision Circular Pomodoro:** Customizable 25-minute deep focus, 5-minute short break, and 15-minute long break intervals. Driven by SVG circular stroke dashoffset trigonometric calculations and Web Audio acoustic chimes.
- **16-Week Daily Activity Heatmap:** GitHub-style study density visualization mapping study sessions, card reviews, and quiz tests. Streak accountability engine reinforcing consistent daily recall routines.
- **Domain Velocity Analytics:** Progress gauges visualizing mastery distribution across Computer Science, Life Sciences, and Applied Mathematics.

---

## Slide 8: Modern Neo-Grotesque UI Philosophy

- **Zero Serif Footprint:** Replaced traditional literary serifs with sharp high-tech typography.
- **Headings & Display:** Outfit (Weights 800/900) with tight -0.04em letter tracking.
- **Interface & Reading:** Plus Jakarta Sans for maximum legibility in dense academic cards.
- **Metrics & Code:** Space Grotesk for countdown timers, score counters, and technical data.
- **Atmospheric Video Background:** Dual ambient background loop with requestAnimationFrame 0.5-second fade-in/fade-out loops.
