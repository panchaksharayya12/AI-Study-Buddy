Problem Statement

The Academic Retention Crisis

70% Loss

Rapid Forgetting Curve

Over 70% of lecture materials and textbook chapters are forgotten within 24 hours without systematic retrieval practice.

15% Efficiency

Passive Reading Illusion

Highlighting and re-reading generate a false sense of familiarity without triggering synaptic consolidation or deep proofs.

4.2 Apps

Tooling Fragmentation

Students juggle disconnected apps for PDFs, notes, timers, and decks, degrading focus and introducing heavy context-switching costs.

Solution Overview

The Active Recall Cognitive Loop

Step 01

Ingest

Upload raw notes, PDFs, and syllabi. Hierarchy parser builds structural chapters and core definitions.

Step 02

Synthesize

Generate smart notes, 3D spaced repetition cards, and adaptive multiple-choice assessments automatically.

Step 03

Reinforce

Flip 3D cards via Leitner intervals, take timed quizzes, and converse with the personal Socratic AI Tutor.

Step 04

Verify

Track retention velocity, daily study heatmaps, domain mastery gauges, and unlockable achievement milestones.

Technical Architecture

Decoupled Reactive Client Stack

Presentation & Motion

Client Layer (Zero Backend Latency)

  • React 18 + Vite 5 + Pure TypeScript compilation.
  • Framer Motion physics curves with requestAnimationFrame loop controller.
  • Procedural Web Audio API synth oscillators (zero external audio file latency).
  • Modern Neo-Grotesque Typography: Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk.
Intelligence & Persistence

Dual-Engine AI Synthesis

  • Direct client-to-API REST stream for Google Gemini Pro (zero proxy overhead).
  • Embedded rule-based heuristic knowledge synthesizer functioning 100% offline.
  • Decoupled React Context Hub (StudyData, Theme, Auth) with LocalStorage sync.
  • Zero learner data harvesting or external analytics tracking.
Capability Deep Dive

Socratic AI Tutor & Ingestion Studio

Socratic Mentorship Dialogue

Rather than simply giving away direct answers, the AI Tutor guides students through deductive reasoning, conceptual analogies, and step-by-step mathematical proofs.

  • Multi-turn conversational context awareness.

Smart Synthesized Notes

Transforms massive reading assignments into structured executive summaries and collated sections with inline keyword highlights.

Active Recall Suite

3D Tactile Cards & Adaptive Quizzing

3D Spaced Repetition Cards

  • Hardware-accelerated CSS 3D perspective transforms with 180° backface flipping.
  • 4-Tier Leitner response ratings: Again, Hard, Good, Easy.
  • Exponential interval scheduling adjusting card review frequency.
  • Full keyboard navigation: Space to flip, Arrow keys to traverse, 1-4 to rate.

Adaptive Assessment Quizzing

  • Automated exam generation based on ingested domain concepts.
  • Real-time score counters, progress meters, and question duration timers.
  • Contextual insight breakdowns explaining why choices are correct or incorrect.
  • Assessment review histories linked to student retention velocity.

Design Philosophy

Neo-Grotesque & Atmospheric Motion

Typography & Visual Identity

  • Eliminated generic literary serif fonts in favor of modern high-tech typography.
  • Headings: Outfit (Weights 800/900) with tight -0.04em letter tracking.
  • UI Body: Plus Jakarta Sans for clean legibility in dense academic materials.
  • Code & Numbers: Space Grotesk for metrics and timers.

Atmospheric Video & Navigation

  • Dual ambient background loop (Meadowbell anime plate + Aethera overlay).
  • requestAnimationFrame precision 0.5s fade-in/fade-out loop controller.
  • Unified top glass navigation bar eliminating dual-sidebar clutter.
  • Full respect for prefers-reduced-motion accessibility preferences.
